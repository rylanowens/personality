The 51 archetypes

An archetype names only what is genuinely distinctive in a profile — the one or two dimensions that sit clearly away from typical (beyond the disclosed threshold), with their directions. Two strong dimensions give a pair archetype (40 of those), one gives a single-pole archetype (10), and a profile with no strong outlier gets the balanced archetype. Reading someone else's entry below, remember what it is: a summary of two numbers, not a species of person — and every writeup lists costs, because every profile pays for its strengths somewhere.

Dimensions: N Negative Emotionality · E Extraversion · O Openness · A Agreeableness · C Conscientiousness. "High/Low" here means beyond roughly the 66th/34th percentile against adult norms — see the methods page for the exact rules.
