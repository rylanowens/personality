Trait Compass
How it works

A personality profile that shows its work

120 statements, about 25 minutes. You get continuous scores on the five major trait dimensions and all 30 facets beneath them, with honest confidence bands — plus a shareable archetype that never hides the numbers it came from.

What it measures

The Big Five trait dimensions — Negative Emotionality, Extraversion, Openness, Agreeableness, and Conscientiousness — each broken into six facets, using the public-domain IPIP-NEO-120 inventory (Johnson, 2014). Scoring, items, and norms are fully disclosed on the methods page.

What you get

Percentile scores with confidence bands for every dimension and facet, an archetype named for what is genuinely distinctive in your profile, how close you sit to neighboring archetypes, and a development section keyed to your actual scores.

What it is not

Not a hiring or selection tool, and not a clinical instrument — it measures ordinary trait differences, not disorders. Your result is an estimate from one sitting, not a permanent identity: scores drift with mood, context, and age, and that is expected.

Your data stays yours

Everything runs in your browser. Answers and results are never sent to any server. Results are saved on this device only, plus a share link that you control.

Answer as the person you actually are — not who you hope to be, and not who you are on your worst day. There are no right answers and nobody to impress.
